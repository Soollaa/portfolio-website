01 / Profile

Satyam Pathak is a creative professional with a passion for visual storytelling, photography, wildlife, and the world of fashion and media. His work reflects creativity, discipline, and a strong eye for visual detail. From professional appearances and the Mister Nepal platform to wildlife photography and camera-based fieldwork, Satyam continues to explore different forms of visual expression. His wildlife work focuses on capturing authentic moments in nature, with a long-term vision of developing wildlife documentaries and meaningful visual stories. Mister Nepal 2024 & 2025 are also part of his journey and experience in the fashion and media space.

His aesthetic combines tailored style, cinematic portraiture and a calm, confident presence in front of the camera.
